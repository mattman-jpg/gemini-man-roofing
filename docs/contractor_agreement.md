# INDEPENDENT SUBCONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (50/50 PROFIT SHARE)

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made effective as of [DATE], by and between **GEMINI MAN ROOFING** ("Company") and **[CONTRACTOR NAME]** ("Subcontractor").

## 1. Scope of Work

Subcontractor agrees to perform roofing installation, repair, and related services for leads provided by the Company.

## 2. Compensation & Profit Share

* **Profit Split:** Net Profit on each job shall be split **50% to Company** and **50% to Subcontractor**.
* **Calculation:** "Net Profit" = (Total Job Revenue) - (Materials + Labor + Permit Fees).
* **Payment Terms:** Payment is due to Subcontractor within 3 days of Company receiving final payment from the property owner/insurance.

## 3. Lead Ownership

All leads provided by Company remain the exclusive property of Gemini Man Roofing. Subcontractor agrees not to solicit these clients for future work for a period of 24 months.

## 4. Quality Standards

Subcontractor guarantees all workmanship for a period of 5 years. Any callbacks or repairs due to poor workmanship are the sole financial responsibility of the Subcontractor.

## 5. Insurance

Subcontractor must maintain General Liability Insurance ($1M minimum) and Workers Compensation as required by Texas law.
