# NON-SOLICITATION & NON-COMPETE ADDENDUM

**1. Non-Solicitation of Clients**
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Subcontractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, induce, or attempt to induce any Client of Gemini Man Roofing to:
(a) Terminate their relationship with the Company;
(b) Transact roofing business directly with the Subcontractor;
(c) Reduce the volume of business they conduct with the Company.

**2. "Client" Definition**
"Client" includes any homeowner, property manager, or commercial entity whose contact information was provided to the Subcontractor by Gemini Man Roofing.

**3. Liquidated Damages**
Subcontractor acknowledges that a breach of this clause causes irreparable harm. Subcontractor agrees to pay a penalty of **$5,000 per violation** immediately upon evidence of solicitation.
