**Step 2: Opt-In Workflow**

**Q: Describe how users opt-in to receive messages from your business.**
A: Users opt-in by visiting our website (geminimanroofing.com) and completing the "Request Inspection" or "Contact Us" form. Specifically, they must satisfy a mandatory checkbox that states: "I agree to receive SMS notifications from Gemini Man Roofing (e.g., appointment confirmations, arrival alerts, weather updates) at the phone number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." This checkbox is required to submit the form. We also obtain verbal consent during recorded inbound calls for service estimates.
