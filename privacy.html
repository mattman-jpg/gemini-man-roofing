Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Gemini Man Roofing ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us via our "Report Damage" or "Contact" forms, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address
  • Information about property damage (e.g., hail size)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and perform roof inspections.
  • Communicate with you about your request, standard services, and emergency repairs.
  • Send storm alerts or relevant follow-up information (you may opt-out at any time).
  • Comply with legal obligations.
