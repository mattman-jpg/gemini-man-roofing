SIGNS OF HIDDEN DAMAGE

Do you see any of these indicators on your property?

🌑

Bruised Shingles

Dark spots where granules have been knocked off. This exposes the asphalt to UV rays, leading to rapid failure.

🌧️

Dented Gutters

If your gutters or downspouts have dents, it's a 99% guarantee that your shingles took hits too.

🍃

Shredded Foliage

Check the trees and bushes around your house. Freshly stripped leaves are a sign of heavy impact.
